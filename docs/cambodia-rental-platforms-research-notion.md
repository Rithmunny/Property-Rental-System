## Executive Summary

The Cambodian rental market is **fragmented** — no single platform dominates daily discovery the way Zillow or PropertyGuru do in other countries. In Phnom Penh, people typically combine **Facebook groups** (fastest, most live listings), **realestate.com.kh** (largest web inventory + market data), and **Khmer24** (Khmer-first, high volume).

**PRS's competitive edge** is not raw listing count — it's the **full rental lifecycle**: search → save → request viewing → contract → **ABA QR payments** → tenant/landlord dashboards. No major incumbent owns that end-to-end experience today.

### Key numbers (Phnom Penh, 2025–2026)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Median asking rent (portals) | ~$750–800/month |
| Typical residential range | $450–1,800/month |
| Listings on realestate.com.kh (PP) | 30,000+ |
| Property ads on Khmer24 (all types) | 75,000+ |
| Hottest districts | BKK1, Chamkarmon, Toul Kork |

## 1. How Phnom Penh Renters Actually Find Homes

| Channel | Role in daily life | Typical user |
|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Facebook groups** | Primary discovery; new listings every hour | Expats, agents, some locals |
| **realestate.com.kh** | Largest web inventory + agent support | Expats, investors, serious renters |
| **Khmer24** | High volume; Khmer + budget/mid-market | Locals; agents cross-posting |
| **Agency portals** (IPS, Compass, Century 21) | Curated listings + human agent | Expats wanting hand-holding |
| **Direct / anti-agent sites** (Rentals Cambodia) | Trust + no commission narrative | Budget-conscious; direct landlords |
| **Emerging map-first** (CamboRent) | Solves "where is this actually?" pain | Locals + expats tired of Facebook |

### Critical insight

Websites are often used for **price research and browsing**. **Facebook + in-person viewings** close most deals. Listings go stale quickly — good BKK1 units can disappear within days.

### What renters repeatedly need

- Honest **map location** (not vague "near BKK1")
- **Total monthly cost** (rent + electricity + water + parking + condo fee)
- **Fresh listings** with posted/updated dates
- **Khmer + English** interface
- **Sangkat / commune** clarity for directions
- Choice of **direct landlord vs agent**
- **FPCS registration** guidance for foreigners
- **Neighbourhood lifestyle** context before committing
- Path from viewing → contract → paying rent (currently fragmented)

## 4. Platform-by-Platform Analysis

### Realestate.com.kh — the benchmark portal

**URL:** https://www.realestate.com.kh/rent/phnom-penh/

**Strengths**
- Deepest inventory and market intelligence (median rent, $/m², price shortcuts)
- Rich filters: furnished, AC, balcony, elevator, WiFi, virtual tours
- Map search with 30K+ pins in Phnom Penh
- Save search; sort by price, area, date
- Landmark search (AEON, Russian Market, BKK1, airport)
- Rental guides and FPCS support for foreigners
- Project/building pages with unit tables

**Weaknesses**
- Agent-centric — "Contact" usually means agent, not owner
- Many stale listings (industry-wide problem)
- Heavy UI; overwhelming for first-time local renters
- Khmer experience secondary to English/expat focus

**Best for:** Price research, expats wanting agent help, new developments

---

### Khmer24 — volume king

**URL:** https://www.khmer24.com/c-property-housing-rentals

**Strengths**
- Khmer-first UI; trusted daily habit for Cambodians
- Massive cross-category reach
- Verified accounts; major agents post heavily
- Province + district filters; price bands

**Weaknesses**
- Not rental-specific — noisy UX
- Weak map; location often vague until you call
- Mixed listing quality; duplicate/agent spam
- No lifecycle tools (payments, contracts, alerts)

**Best for:** Locals, budget hunting, agents maximizing exposure

---

### IPS Cambodia & Compass.com.kh — agency portals

**URLs:** https://ips-cambodia.com/ · https://www.compass.com.kh/rent-listing

**Strengths**
- Professional photos; structured property codes
- Advanced search (province, sangkat, commercial/residential)
- IPS: property management, valuation, corporate services
- Compass: landmark-based search, rental encyclopedia/guides

**Weaknesses**
- Smaller self-serve inventory vs realestate.com.kh
- Compass UI feels dated; mixed EN/CN
- Agent-mediated — slower for casual browsing

**Best for:** Relocating employees, families, commercial space

---

### Rent KH — rental specialist

**URL:** https://rentkh.com/search

**Strengths**
- Rental-only categories (apartment, villa, warehouse, room rent)
- Map view; multi-unit buildings with per-room pricing
- Amenity tags (24/7 security, furnished, parking)
- "Book to visit" CTA

**Weaknesses**
- Smaller brand vs realestate.com.kh
- Limited trust/verification story
- No tenant account lifecycle

**Best for:** Building-level rentals, commercial/warehouse

---

### Rentals Cambodia — trust & direct contact

**URL:** https://www.rentalscambodia.com/

**Strengths**
- Clear promise: no agent fees, direct owner contact
- Human-verified listings — strong trust signal
- Organized by sangkat (matches local mental model)
- Phone, Telegram, WhatsApp on every listing
- Simple $10 one-time landlord fee

**Weaknesses**
- Very small inventory (~9 listings nationally at time of research)
- Limited filters; no deep map yet
- No in-app rental management

**Best for:** Renters avoiding agent markup; landlords avoiding finder fees

---

### CamboRent — upcoming map-first competitor

**URL:** https://camborent.com/ *(Launching Soon)*

**Strengths (public positioning)**
- Draw your area on the map — unique vs incumbents
- Bilingual EN/KH throughout
- Report inaccurate photos/prices/addresses
- Covers residential + commercial + hospitality + industrial
- Explicitly targets Facebook Marketplace pain points

**Weaknesses**
- Not yet a live market (demo uses ~1,600 mock listings)

**Best for (future):** Anyone frustrated by hidden pins and wrong-neighbourhood Facebook posts

---

### Facebook groups — real daily-life UX

**Key groups:** Phnom Penh Housing · Phnom Penh Shared Housing · Apartment For Rent In Phnom Penh · Digital Nomads Cambodia · Expats in Cambodia

**Strengths**
- Fastest updates; real landlords and agents
- Negotiation culture; post requirements and get DMs
- Photos, video walkthroughs, immediate Messenger chat
- Shared housing / roommate sub-market

**Weaknesses**
- No structured filters, map, or price history
- Scams and stale posts; location hidden until you ask
- No payment tracking, contracts, or FPCS reminders
- Overwhelming for newcomers

**Best for:** Active hunters already in Phnom Penh; 1–2 week in-person search

## 7. Strategic Positioning

**Recommended positioning for PRS:**

> *"The rental platform that takes you from search to signed lease to monthly rent — built for Phnom Penh daily life."*

Do **not** compete on raw listing count (30K vs seed data). Compete on **trust + neighbourhood clarity + payments + structured requests** — the parts Facebook and portals don't finish.

- [ ] "Posted / updated" dates on every listing

- [ ] Draw-area map filter (CamboRent feature)
